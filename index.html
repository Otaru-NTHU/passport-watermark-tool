🔳 MRZ Masking 機器可讀區遮蔽

MRZ (Machine Readable Zone) is the two-line code at the bottom of your passport. It contains the same information as the top section (name, passport number, nationality, etc.) in machine-readable format. Masking it adds extra protection without affecting most use cases.
MRZ 是護照底部的兩行編碼，內容與上方資訊重複（姓名、護照號碼、國籍等）。遮蔽後不影響大多數用途，但可提供額外保護。

Why watermark your passport copy? 為什麼要加浮水印？

🛡️

Prevent Misuse 防止盜用

A watermarked copy cannot easily be repurposed for identity theft, fraudulent account creation, or unauthorized applications.
加上浮水印的影本難以被挪用於身份盜竊或冒名申請。

📌

Limit Scope 限定用途

The watermark clearly states the intended use, so anyone who sees the copy knows it was provided for a specific purpose only.
浮水印清楚標明用途，讓人知道此影本僅限特定目的使用。

🔳

MRZ Masking 編碼遮蔽

The machine-readable zone duplicates your personal data. Masking it prevents automated scanning while keeping visual info intact.
MRZ 重複了你的個資，遮蔽後可防止自動掃描，同時保持視覺資訊完整。
